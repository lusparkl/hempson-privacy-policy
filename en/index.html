Hepson

AI sales assistant for online shops

Hepson is a service for online shops. A shop connects its own Instagram professional account (using the Instagram API with Instagram Login) and its website chat, and later a Telegram bot. An AI sales assistant then answers customers' messages and helps them choose products from the shop's catalog.

The assistant collects order details (name, phone, city, delivery branch) and passes the order to the shop owner for approval. The shop owner can take over any conversation at any time. Hepson is operated by Ivashchenko Oleksander, an individual developer, and is currently in a pilot stage.
