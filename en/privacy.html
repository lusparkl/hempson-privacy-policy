Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Hepson is an AI sales assistant for online shops, operated by Ivashchenko Oleksander, an individual developer ("Hepson", "we"). This policy explains what personal data Hepson processes, why, and what rights you have. Contact: flypyfl@gmail.com.

1. Who is responsible for the data

  • Customers' data. When a customer messages a shop, the shop is the data controller. Hepson acts as the processor and handles this data only on the shop's instructions.
  • Shop owners' account data. For the data of shop owners who register in Hepson, Hepson is the controller.

2. Data we collect

From shop owners

  • email address and hashed password;
  • shop settings and product catalog;
  • Instagram professional account ID and username;
  • Instagram access token. It is stored encrypted and is never shown or written to logs.

From customers who message a shop

  • Instagram-scoped user ID;
  • username or profile name, when available;
  • message text and timestamps;
  • order details the customer provides: name, phone, city, delivery branch and chosen items.

3. Why we use the data

  • replying to customers' messages;
  • preparing orders and passing them to the shop owner;
  • showing conversations to the shop owner, who can take over any conversation;
  • security and abuse prevention.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not build profiles beyond the conversation itself.

4. Third parties

Data is shared with the following providers only as far as needed, and each one is used only when the shop enables the related feature or connection:

  • Meta (Instagram messaging) – to receive and send Instagram messages for the connected account.
  • OpenAI – an AI model provider that receives conversation text in order to generate replies.
  • Nova Poshta – receives delivery data (for example city and branch) when the shop uses this integration.
  • Monobank and WayForPay – payment providers, used when the shop enables online payment.

5. Security

  • Instagram access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Access to a shop's data is limited to the members of that shop.
  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect data.

6. Retention

Customer conversations and order details are kept for 14 days, then deleted or anonymised, unless the shop deletes them earlier. The shop owner's account data is kept while the account exists. Data of a disconnected Instagram account is deleted on request (see Data Deletion Instructions).

7. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection" you may request access to your data, correction, deletion, and object to its processing. To use these rights, email flypyfl@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. If your data was given to a shop, we may need to coordinate your request with that shop as the controller. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The service is not directed to people under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top and in the footer shows the current version.

10. Contact
