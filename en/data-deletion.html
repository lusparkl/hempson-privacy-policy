Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Hepson does not have an automated deletion endpoint. Deletion requests are handled manually by email, as described below.

If you are a customer who messaged a shop

  1. Email flypyfl@gmail.com.
  2. Include your Instagram username and the name of the shop you wrote to.

Alternatively, you can ask the shop itself to delete your data by messaging it.

If you are a shop owner

  1. In the Hepson dashboard open Settings → Instagram → Disconnect. This revokes and deletes the stored Instagram access token.
  2. Email flypyfl@gmail.com and ask us to delete the remaining data (conversations, orders, catalog, account).

What happens next

  • Data is deleted within 30 days of your request.
  • We send a confirmation by email when deletion is complete.

Conversations and order details are in any case deleted or anonymised after 14 days, as described in the Privacy Policy.
